## Executive findings

1. **Top Scholar scripts each audit the prompt's key operator and turn it into a thesis they own.**
   - 2022: "The question itself is flawed", because empowerment is not equal footing.
   - 2024: willingness does not equal ability.
   - 2025: the dispute is really about truth and identity, not about social-justice ends.
   - 2023: "for whom?" is answered as "for humanity, because religiousness is universal".
2. **Outstanding scripts are coverage-driven.** They run a typology (3 women, 4 psychologists, 3 typologies, 6 DEI cells) and assess each unit competently. They tend to end on "both sides" or a mood.
3. **Scholarship-tier scripts are topic parades.** Cases are labelled as "examples of" a model rather than tested against it. Counter-views appear as one-sentence "However, X argues…" rebuttals that nobody adjudicates.
4. **Originality lives in how a case is used, not in which case is chosen.** The most-used cases are canonical: Gen 3, Galatians 3:28, Galileo, Saul on the Damascus road, *Imago Dei*. Top scripts pull a claim out of each case that changes the argument.
5. **Marker language for the mid-tier repeats.** The phrases are "engagement with counter arguments", "more specific studies", "inadequately defended generalisation" and "relatively simple but logical".
6. **Fancy vocabulary is rewarded only when it names a distinction** (submission vs oppression; paradigm vs worldview; soft vs hard equity). Ornamental abstraction shows up in Outstanding-tier writing and is where the weakest sentences sit.
7. **Conclusions do not need to be quote-free, but they must contain a stance.** Bacon, Milton and Pope Francis closers appear in high-scoring scripts. The scripts that stopped at the quote (2024 Outstanding; 2023 Outstanding's "mystery") lost the last marks.
8. **A second-tradition comparator earns its place only when it does argumentative work.** Al-Ghazali in the 2024 Top script is the decisive contrast. The Roman / Egyptian / Hindu goddess list in the 2022 Scholarship script is ornament.
9. **2025 shows convergence.** Five of five scripts are Catholic-centred, and the same cluster recurs (*Imago Dei*, Gal 3:28, *Ordinatio Sacerdotalis*, *Fiducia Supplicans*, Boff, Fiorenza). When the case set is shared, the framework becomes the differentiator.

## 1. Structural archetype and macro-architecture

### 1.1 The three archetypes

| | **A. Lens-driven thesis** (Top) | **B. Typology / coverage** (Outstanding) | **C. Case parade** (Scholarship) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spine | One analytical lens applied to the prompt itself | 3–6 parallel units under a shared template | List of cases and figures under a named model |
| Examples | 2022 Hegel; 2024 Kuhn; 2023 perennialist/constructivist → Haidt; 2025 Levinas/Foucault/Durkheim | 2022 Eve–Mary–Andal; 2023 Freud–Jung–James–Fowler; 2024 3 typologies; 2025 D/E/I × acted/contradicted | 2022 Bible → ordination → complementarianism → Gloriavale; 2024 Scopes → Dawkins → Vatican Observatory → Sagan → *Laudato Si'* → Collins |
| Where the argument lives | In one contestable claim carried across sections | In each unit's mini-verdict, plus a final tally | In the intro and conclusion, which restate each other |
| Failure mode | Over-tidy synthesis; some apologetic tilt | Ends on "both sides"; strands the best idea | Never adjudicates |

### 1.2 Thesis mechanics

Top scripts state a thesis that both answers the prompt and reinterprets its key term.

- **2022 Top.** "There is a long tradition… of oppression… an equally long tradition of liberation." The candidate promises that the "apparent contradiction is no contradiction at all". The tension is stated as a paradox to be dissolved, and the lens (Hegelian dialectic over *la longue durée*) is named in paragraph 1. The planning page had already asked: "does power give 'equal footing'?" and "is this the desired outcome?"
- **2024 Top.** Paragraph 1 says willingness "does not equal ability". This is a caveat built into the thesis itself, so the marker cannot raise it as an objection.
- **2025 Top.** The intro compresses the dispute to one word, "identity", then promises three lettered sections (A/B/C).
- **2023 Top.** Paragraph 1 fixes the claim (universality is convincing and useful), and paragraph 2 names the theorists who will carry it (James, Proudfoot, Haidt, Freud).

Outstanding scripts state thesis-plus-scope but not an operator audit.
- 2022 Outstanding says "The key term here is 'significant progress'", then defines it.
- 2024 Outstanding declares "3 scientific typologies" and a Western-Christian scope.
- 2025 Outstanding opens on an "Fanon vs Fiorenza vs Holland" paradox. Its thesis (a "restless struggle") is not stated until page 8.

Scholarship-tier intros restate the prompt, list the cases, and add dictionary definitions.
- 2024 Scholarship: "Science can be defined by the dictionary as…".
- 2022 Scholarship: "Take Christianity, for example."

### 1.3 Scaffolding and routing

- **Roadmap sentences.** Named in Outstanding and Top scripts (2022 Top "This will be discussed in the antithesis"; 2025 Top "A) … B) … C)"; 2024 Outstanding numbered typologies).
- **Visible headers.** The two 2025 scripts with lettered or numbered sections (Top, and `scholarship.md`'s "Section One/Two/Three") read as more navigable than unheaded ones. Headers alone do not lift a mark: the 2025 `scholarship.md` script has headers but no stated thesis or conclusion in the transcription.
- **Hinge sentences.** These convert one section's result into the next section's question:
  - 2025 Top: "the dispute goes beyond actions, but is rooted in different beliefs around why".
  - 2024 Outstanding: "Is there, therefore, a way for the profound answering ability… to be kept?"
- **Planning pages.**
  - Top scripts plan claims and tensions: 2022 Top's dialectic table and pre-selected verbatim quotes; 2024 Top's "Lost cause? Evasion to the real debate?"
  - Scholarship scripts plan topics: 2022 Scholarship "P1: Bible… P5: case study Gloriavale… P6: conclusions"; 2024 Scholarship's list of names. The 2025 `scholarship.md` script left its planning blank.

### 1.4 Paragraph-level engineering (the Outstanding-grade paragraph)

The most reproducible unit is in the 2023 Top script, repeated three times:

1. **Position.** State what the theorist claims (James's four hallmarks).
2. **Test.** Run it on a concrete case (Saul, Acts 9: transient, passive, noetic).
3. **Rival.** Give the best counter, with its mechanism (Proudfoot's "problem of intentionality", illustrated with Bush's late-bus example).
4. **Counter-rival.** Point out what the rival omits (Bush's school-bully example: pain arrives before any concept of it).
5. **Adjudicate.** Deliver a partial verdict ("nothing… has been able to disprove" the perennialist core).

The tell is the phrase "The assumption that this argument makes however is…", used for Proudfoot, Haidt and others: each source is identified by the assumption it needs.

2024 Outstanding uses a shorter version: claim, then a named thinker, then a named critic, then the "caveat". That is competent but shallower. The theorist is stated and the critic appears, but the cases are not really tested. For example, the Sam Harris section as transcribed seems to invert his position: it says he argued loss of belief means loss of morality. That is the opposite of his usual view, though it may be transcription or a slip.

### 1.5 Pivots and nuance management

- **Contrast-by-controlled-pair (2024 Top).** Galileo and Al-Ghazali are both tests of "willingness to question". One fails and one succeeds. The essay then argues the difference is contingent institutional conditions, not the religion itself: "it's not questioning that is difficult, but the answering." Creationism follows as a stress test.
- **Criterion carried across blocks (2024 Outstanding).** Ratzinger's "profound questions of meaning" test is applied first to sociological reductions of religion, then to Polkinghorne's natural theology, then to miracles. This is the essay's best architectural feature and could have been developed much further.
- **Thesis → antithesis → question-critique (2022 Top).** After the dialectic, the essay splits the prompt into (A) "empowering" and (B) "equal footing" and answers each separately: no and partial yes.
- **Pro → local example → "Yet" → con → local example (2025 Outstanding).** Each of the three DEI terms gets both sides, with an Aotearoa example each time. Consistent, but every "Yet" produces the same cost/benefit shape.
- **Pair-and-rebut (2024 Scholarship).** Each figure is followed by one rebutting figure: Dawkins/Plantinga, Sagan/Craig, Collins/Harris. Pivots exist, but none adjudicates.

### 1.6 Originality of case studies

**Verdict:** Case originality by itself is not what separates the tiers. What separates them is what each case *proves*.

- **Canonical cases dominate the whole range.** Genesis 3, Galatians 3:28, Galileo, Saul on the Damascus road, *Imago Dei* and MLK appear at every tier.
- **Top scripts extract a claim that would not exist without the case.**
  - Galileo → "the Church did test Galileo; the problem was the paradigm's beholdenness".
  - Al-Ghazali → "a religious critique using his opponents' own principles, pre-empting Kuhn".
  - Saul → tests whether James's four hallmarks are met.
  - Joan of Arc / Isabella / Mother Teresa → distinguishes submission from oppression.
- **Low-tier scripts stop at "this shows the need for dialogue".** Scopes (2024) and the Vatican Observatory yield only that platitude. Gloriavale (2022, and again in the 2025 `scholarship.md` script) is used as a symptom, with no engagement with why it might be an outlier.
- **NZ or local cases add value when they carry the argument, and can backfire when they do not.**
  - Good: Christchurch 2019 (2025 Top), the Royal Commission into Abuse in Care (2025 Outstanding).
  - Weak: "Britomart station's rebrand to Waitematā, which speaks to an adapting embrace of inclusion" in the 2025 Outstanding script. A transport rename is not a Catholic act, so it is a non-sequitur.
- **Unusual comparators help if they are contrasts, not lists.** Al-Ghazali (2024 Top) is the decisive one. Andal (2022 Outstanding) supplies the scale-of-progress contrast. The goddess list in the 2022 Scholarship conclusion (which also lists Jupiter among "female" gods) does not.

**Takeaway.** Use canonical cases without apology, but have each one settle a specific question for the argument. Reach for an unusual case only when it works as a contrast that changes the result.

### 1.7 Vocabulary

**Verdict:** Precision helps; ornament does not. The marker comment for the 2022 Top Scholar credits "sophisticated vocabulary" *together with* "precise philosophical distinction between power and spiritual equality". The vocabulary was rewarded because it named a distinction.

- **Vocabulary that did work.**
  - *incommensurable*, *paradigm*, *aporia*, *hermeneutical* (2024 Top).
  - *submission vs oppression* (2022 Top).
  - "soft equity" vs "hard equity" (2025 Top).
  - *religiousness* as a defined term that supports a universality claim (2023 Top).
- **Vocabulary that was decoration.**
  - The 2025 Outstanding script has lines like "diversity is not obscene, nor a threat but sacramental". A few phrases are garbled: "best-value advocacy of equity against internal suppression of young causes".
  - The 2024 Top script has tangled sentences in places (partly transcription).
- **Plain style is safe.** The 2025 `scholarship.md` and 2024 Scholarship scripts are plain. Plain style did not cap them below 13 or 14. Their limits came from analysis, not diction.
- **Register slips cost credibility** (2022 Scholarship's "God is a woman" closing).

**Takeaway.** Use one or two technical terms per section, define each on first use, and reuse it in the conclusion. Do not sound "fancy" as a goal.

## 2. What top performers consistently get right

### 2.1 Interlocking frameworks

- **2022 Top.** Aquinas and Augustine (whether submission is innate) are set against Luther (it follows the Fall). That contrast is then used to explain why Baptists and Anglicans ordain but Catholics do not. Scripture, dogma and poetry (Milton) are tied to a single dialectic.
- **2023 Top.** James vs Proudfoot vs Bush is one conversation. Freud enters only to test Haidt's "unity" claim, and Haidt survives by reinterpreting refuge as unity.
- **2024 Top.** Kuhn is used to reread Galileo, Al-Ghazali and creationism as one phenomenon. The author also cross-links Gould (non-overlapping magisteria) as a target.
- **2024 Outstanding.** Ratzinger's criterion is threaded through three blocks, though the interlock is thinner than in Top scripts.
- **Weak comparator: 2024 Scholarship.** Barbour's taxonomy is announced but never used to sort the cases.

### 2.2 Evidence density and precision

- **Top scripts cite title, date and locator.**
  - 2025 Top: *Totality and Infinity* (1961), *Centesimus Annus* (1991), Judges 21:25.
  - 2024 Top: Psalm 104, Joshua, Surah 39, Augustine's *De Genesi ad Litteram*, Bellarmine, Trent.
  - 2023 Top: Bush on Proudfoot, Watts on the two schools, Matilal on ineffability.
- **Commentary-to-quote ratio.** Top scripts pair a short verbatim quote with commentary at least twice as long, and the commentary always makes a claim about the quote's function.
- **Scripture volume varies by topic.** 2022 Top (women & religion) is scripture-heavy; 2023 Top (psychology) is secondary-literature-heavy. The variable is not "more quotes", it is "quotes the argument depends on".
- **Mid-tier evidence is broad but thin.** The 2024 Scholarship script has many names and about one sentence of use each.

### 2.3 Caveat architecture

- 2024 Outstanding uses the literal word "caveat" to close each block and states its scope up front.
- 2024 Top builds the caveat into the thesis ("willingness ≠ ability") and repeatedly concedes: "this does not mean his own paradigm was at all free of bias".
- 2023 Top rebuts a critique by naming its source's bias (Freud's own letter: "personal and not psychoanalytic"), then keeps the salvageable point ("Despite these biases…").
- 2022 Outstanding hedges inline ("I say 'patriarchal', although Hinduism is very complicated").

### 2.4 Synthesis vs summary

- **2022 Top:** "This resolves the question's separate parts": two verdicts, each with reasoning.
- **2022 Outstanding:** splits into groundwork (yes) and significant progress (no), and uses a consistent scale-of-reach test for "significant".
- **2025 Top:** ends each section with a one-line inference that becomes the next section's premise.
- **Scholarship-tier scripts:** restate the intro as the conclusion (2024 Scholarship; 2025 `samuelraw`).

## 3. Harsh marker audit

### 3.1 Forced frameworks and shoehorning

| Script | Move | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|
| 2022 Top | "Complementarianism" is the dialectical synthesis | The synthesis is the conservative position in disguise. An opponent would not accept it as the middle. The wheel-and-machine analogy conflates necessity with equal power. |
| 2022 Top | *Paradise Lost* as evidence of "views of contemporary Christians" | A 17th-century poem stands in for Scripture and for doctrine. It is a stretch even as a marker of tradition. |
| 2022 Outstanding | Three women as "indicators" of progress across religions; progress "increased" from Eve to Mary to Andal | The order was the candidate's choice. That is an ordering artefact, not a trend. Andal (8th century) shows historical potential, not modern progress. |
| 2023 Outstanding | Four theorists in a fixed template (weakness / useful / convincing) | The "for whom?" part is thin (atheists / religious / everyone). Some claims are unsupported (an uncited Ramadan study; Fowler's theory leading to "drinking"). Freud is placed in the "18th century" and Freud, Jung and James are called "17th/18th century" psychologists. |
| 2024 Outstanding | Durkheim, Hick, Gould and Lennox grouped as one "complimentary attempt" | These have very different commitments. Hick is not a Durkheimian. The question also drifts from "willingness to question and test" to "willingness to accept caveats". |
| 2024 Scholarship | Barbour's four models announced, then unused | The cases (Scopes = conflict; Vatican Observatory = dialogue) are not sorted. The question asked about poverty and climate cooperation, but only *Laudato Si'* touches it. |
| 2025 Top | DEI = postmodernism = Foucault | This is a contestable caricature. "Poststructuralism" is credited to Foucault via a Saussure/Derrida-style definition. Chomsky's "universal grammar" is offered as a one-line refutation, which is a category error. |
| 2025 `jenoraw` | Newman's "Logical Sequence" test | The four-step syllogism (imago Dei → equality → say → clergy) assumes that "having a say" means "ordination". Only 3 of the 7 notes are used. |
| 2025 Outstanding | Six-cell D/E/I × acted/contradicted matrix | Ensures coverage, but each "Yet" resolves into a formula. |

### 3.2 Hand-waving transitions

- **2022 Outstanding.** "Whether this can be considered 'significant progress' is up for debate" leads straight to "it is just not widespread enough". The reasoning between them is missing.
- **2024 Top.** "Under Kuhnian scientific theory, this is not possible, or is highly improbable" is asserted without demonstration.
- **2025 Top.** "This rings true to an extent" is followed by Nazi Germany in one sentence, with no argument connecting categorisation to totalitarianism.
- **2025 Outstanding.** "the assemblies of the Church… highlighting that their founding doctrines can be literally enforced today" is opaque.
- **2025 Outstanding, unfalsifiable move.** "In post-crises, the contradictions of Catholic tradition do not mark complete failures, but testament to the faith's vitality." As written, any failure counts as evidence of vitality, which makes the claim unfalsifiable.

### 3.3 Conclusion cop-outs

| Script | Closer | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 2022 Scholarship | Daly quote plus Ariana Grande | Register break, no stance. |
| 2023 Outstanding | "religion is a mystery that can never be cracked, only appreciated" | Declines to conclude after asserting "the only fair answer". |
| 2024 Outstanding | "it is hard to end this essay with a definitive conclusion", then Bacon | Explicit cop-out. It undercuts a genuinely original Humean-science idea. |
| 2024 Scholarship | Restates the intro, then Tyson | No adjudication. |
| 2025 Outstanding | Pope Francis quote after "restless struggle" | Stance exists, but it is accommodationist. |
| 2025 `jenoraw` | "I believe God will guide the Church" | A statement of faith, not a conclusion. |
| 2023 Top | Speculates that research may end religious conflict | Overreach. Biological universality does not entail equal truth of all religions. |

**Note.** 2022 Top uses Milton lines *inside* the argument and then closes with two definite verdicts. A quote is not the flaw. The flaw is when the quote replaces the stance.

### 3.4 Depth vs scope trade-offs

- **2024 Outstanding.** About 18 pages spread over three typologies. The strongest idea (Humean science as a way through) gets about two pages.
- **2022 Outstanding.** Andal is the pivot of the whole conclusion and gets about a page. The prompt asked for counter-examples, and the marker asked for more of them.
- **2025 `jenoraw` and `samuelraw`.** Each covers three disputes (inculturation, women, LGBTQ+) plus a lens, and each dispute gets a few paragraphs.
- **2025 `scholarship.md`.** The longest script by far, but its length goes to exegesis and a survey of schools.

**Observation.** The mid-tier tends to sacrifice depth to keep a tripartite structure. The Top scripts keep the structure but spend most of their space on the decisive comparison.

## 4. Master structural pattern matrix

*A reusable template synthesised from Top and Outstanding scripts. It is descriptive of what scored, not a guarantee.*

### 4.1 Introduction protocol (about 200–350 words, 1–2 paragraphs)

| # | Operational goal | Evidence in exemplars |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Name the tension in the prompt (not a quote drop). | 2022 Top: oppression tradition vs liberation tradition. |
| S2 | State the thesis as a claim that redefines or disambiguates the key operator. | 2024 Top: willingness ≠ ability. 2022 Top: "apparent contradiction… no contradiction". |
| S3 | Name the lens and why it fits this prompt. | Hegel (2022), Kuhn (2024), Levinas / Foucault / Durkheim (2025), Newman (`jenoraw`). |
| S4 | Declare scope and its honest limits. | 2022 Top: Christian approach, Catholic focus. 2024 Outstanding: Western-Christian, 2,000 years. |
| S5 | Give the route (2–4 named sections) with each section's job. | 2025 Top: A/B/C. 2022 Top: "discussed in the antithesis". |
| S6 | Define only the terms you will reuse, in operational form. | 2022 Outstanding: "dependent variable". 2023 Top: *religiousness*. Avoid dictionary definitions (2024 Scholarship). |

### 4.2 Body block matrix (5 steps, per theoretical block)

1. **Block claim.** One sentence tying the block to the thesis.
2. **Source and framework.** The thinker or text with a locator (title, year, verse) and a short quote.
3. **Test on a case.** Apply it to a specific case and extract a claim the case supports (Saul → four hallmarks; Galileo → paradigm beholdenness).
4. **Rival and its assumption.** The strongest named counter, phrased as "the assumption this makes is…".
5. **Adjudicate and hinge.** Give a partial verdict with an explicit criterion, then pose the question the next block answers. Carry the criterion forward (Ratzinger's "profound questions of meaning" across 2024 Outstanding's blocks).

**Rules of thumb.**
- Use at most 3 blocks unless a matrix is doing real coverage work.
- Give the decisive block 40% of the space.
- Every case must settle something.

### 4.3 Synthesis and dialectic pivot rules

1. Put the counter after the test, not before.
2. Name the counter's hidden assumption instead of merely opposing it.
3. Adjudicate by a criterion (reach, mechanism, consistency with text), not by "both have a point".
4. Concede scope without collapsing the thesis (Al-Ghazali "not at all free of bias, but…").
5. Cap counters at two per block. Avoid pair-and-rebut lists.
6. A synthesis must be one an opponent would recognise as a fair middle. If it is one side's view relabelled, say so and defend it as such.
7. Use a comparator from another tradition or context only as a contrast that changes the result.
8. Do not let a local example carry a claim it does not support (Waitematā).

### 4.4 Conclusion protocol (about 150–250 words)

1. **Answer.** One sentence that answers the prompt's operator ("groundwork yes; significant progress no").
2. **Decisive block.** Say which block did the work and why.
3. **Strongest remaining objection.** State it and say whether it overturns, limits, or leaves the claim standing.
4. **Bounded implication.** Only what follows from the argument, with no leaps (avoid 2023 Top's world-peace ending).
5. **Optional closing line.** A quote only if it adds argument and follows the stance.

**Avoid:**
- restating the intro;
- "hard to end definitively";
- "mystery" endings;
- pop-culture lines;
- personal faith declarations as conclusions;
- unfalsifiable framing (any failure = vitality);
- **false analogy** (wheel and machine);
- **begging the question** ("submission is honour");
- **non-sequitur** (Britomart);
- **ordering artefact** as a trend;
- **appeal to reach** as a test of truth;
- **single-case generalisation**;
- **category error** (Chomsky vs Foucault).

## 6. Council verdict

*Method note: the council skill calls for five parallel sub-agents. That capability is not available in this chat, so I ran the five lenses as separate reasoning passes, anonymised them for peer review, and synthesised as chairman. Treat this as structured self-critique, not five independent instances.*

**Framed question.** From this corpus, what should a Scholarship RS candidate actually do to maximise marks? In particular: (1) do original case studies matter, (2) does sophisticated vocabulary help, (3) is reframing the prompt worth the risk, and (4) breadth or depth?

**Advisors (condensed).**
- **Contrarian.** The sample is 14 scripts and 4 Top Scholars. Markers' comments are 2–3 sentences. Top scripts may simply be by stronger students. Many flaws I found cost nothing (2022 Top's synthesis is arguably a smuggled position and still scored 24/24). 2025 has no scores. Transcription noise inflates some "flaws".
- **First Principles.** The markers reward "an argument of one's own that survives its best objection", shown by perceptive comprehension, independent reflection and logical development. Everything else (cases, vocabulary, headers) is a means. Ask what each paragraph does for that argument.
- **Expansionist.** The operator-audit move is the highest-leverage repeatable technique, and it is teachable: rewrite the prompt's key term as a distinction and defend it. A second-tradition contrast case is an underused multiplier.
- **Outsider.** Reading cold, I can state the Top arguments in one sentence after the intro. The Outstanding essays read like competent surveys; the Scholarship ones read like book reports. Plain sentences beat elaborate ones. The pop-culture closer jars.
