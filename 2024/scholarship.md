It is important to know the difference between compatible and complementarity as they are vastly different but often get confused with each other. Ian Barbour's taxonomy consists of 4 models:

1. **The conflict model** (which says that science and religion are incompatible / do not cross over).


2. **The independent model** (which says that science and religion are 2 different fields that ask different questions, not compatible nor conflicting).


3. **The dialogue model** (which says that there is some overlap between science and religion and there is value in dialogue).


4. **The interdependent model** (which says that science and religion fully cross over and rely on each other).
